TL;DR核心摘要（TL;DR）

We re-ran the experiments in Takehi et al.’s “Sparks of Weight-Space Programming: LLMs can natively read & write neural network weights” using the authors’ released logs, checkpoints, and prompts (commit 4d09ba6), and added no-LLM scripts, hyperparameter-tuned RL baselines, multi-reply reliability runs, and open-weight model comparisons. 基于作者公开的日志、模型权重与提示词（提交 4d09ba6），我们复现了 Takehi 等人 “Sparks of Weight-Space Programming: LLMs can natively read & write neural network weights”（《权重空间编程的火花：大语言模型可原生读写神经网络权重》） 中的实验，并补充了无 LLM 脚本基线、超参数调优强化学习基线、多次采样可靠性实验以及开源权重模型对比。

Every reported score we re-ran reproduces, and GPT-6 Astra has real, specific abilities that current open-weight models lack: it reads obvious structure in small networks (Experiment 1: 12/12 on game ID and 12/12 abstention on random weights; Experiment 2: 9/9 on shape names in 3× reruns, while DeepSeek V4.1, Kimi K3, and GLM 5.3 fail or hallucinate on these reading tasks; Experiment 3: 6/6 runs finding the 0.2 induction-head set by chaining weight-algebra steps) and compiles explicit programs into weights (Experiment 5 Breakout median 490 over 10 replies, with 9/10 reaching 90% of the authors’ network score in our evaluation; Asterix median 62.5 over 10 replies, with 7/10 reaching that threshold; Experiment 6 Go networks b0/b50 beating our 30-line tactical script 90–95% head-to-head). 我们重跑的所有报告分数均得以复现，且 GPT-6 Astra 具备当前开源权重模型所不具备的特定真实能力：它能够读取小型网络中的显著结构（实验 1：游戏识别 12/12 且随机权重 12/12 正确拒答；实验 2：3 倍重跑形状名称 9/9 全对，而 DeepSeek V4.1、Kimi K3 与 GLM 5.3 在这些读取任务上失败或产生幻觉；实验 3：6/6 次运行通过串联权重代数步骤找出 0.2 阈值复制头集合），并能将给定程序编译进神经网络权重（实验 5 中 Breakout 的 10 次回复中位数为 490，9/10 达到作者网络在我们评测中得分的 90%；Asterix 的 10 次回复中位数为 62.5，7/10 达到该阈值；实验 6 围棋网络 b0/b50 在对阵我们的 30 行战术脚本时胜率达 90–95%）。

However, the headline comparisons rest on weak or untuned baselines, single selected runs, and simulator source code in the prompt: 然而，原博客最引人注目的对比结论依赖于薄弱或未调优的基线、单次筛选的运行结果，以及提示词中直接提供的模拟器源码：

  • Weak or handicapped baselines (Experiments 5, 6, 8): In Experiment 5, tuning PPO (512 envs vs CleanRL’s 64, lr 1e-3, 500M steps) lifts Seaquest to 99.2–114.4 (about 2× Astra’s 53.1) and Breakout to 56.1–440.1 (shrinking Astra’s lead from 20× to 1.1–9×). In Experiment 6, a fixed 30-line, 1-ply tactics script R1 with no network or search beats the equal-sized PPO networks 74.1% / 61.1% / 69.3% (more than Astra b0’s 42.6% / 26.3% / 36.3%). In Experiment 8, unedited Qwen3.5-0.8B with thinking off already scores 44.2% on MATH-500, 56.0% on GSM8K, and 54.7% on IFEval; against the best no-edit decoding setting, Astra’s edit adds +6.4 on MATH-500, +11.5 on GSM8K, and −2.2 on IFEval, while a textbook difference-of-means direction with no LLM reaches 41.9% on MATH-500 (recovering 79% of Astra’s 8.3% → 50.6% gain; random-direction and random-column controls score 5.1% and 8.0%). 薄弱或受限的基线（实验 5、6、8）：在实验 5 中，调优 PPO（512 环境对比 CleanRL 默认的 64 环境、学习率 1e-3、500M 步）将 Seaquest 提升至 99.2–114.4（约为 Astra 53.1 的 2×），将 Breakout 提升至 56.1–440.1（使 Astra 的领先幅度从 20× 缩小至 1.1–9×）。在实验 6 中，一个不含网络与搜索的 30 行固定 1 步战术脚本 R1 对阵同尺寸 PPO 网络的胜率即达 74.1% / 61.1% / 69.3%（高于 Astra b0 的 42.6% / 26.3% / 36.3%）。在实验 8 中，未编辑的 Qwen3.5-0.8B 在关闭思考模式下于 MATH-500、GSM8K 和 IFEval 上已达 44.2%、56.0% 和 54.7%；相比最佳无编辑解码设置，Astra 的编辑在 MATH-500 上仅增 +6.4、GSM8K 上增 +11.5、IFEval 上为 −2.2，而无需 LLM 的教科书式均值差方向在 MATH-500 上即可达 41.9%（复现 Astra 从 8.3% 到 50.6% 增益的 79%；随机方向与随机列对照仅为 5.1% 和 8.0%）。

In Experiment 4, a 2-parameter open-loop schedule (reverse 16 steps, then drive) nearly solves the task open-loop. On the coarse grid, both achieve 256/256 for start shifts ±0.5, friction ×0.8–1.2, and body mass ×0.5–4; the finer friction sweep extends the match to ×0.7–1.8 at 16/16 per point. Under the stronger observation-noise settings, open-loop achieves 256/256 versus the edit’s 252–253/256. At the tested edge points (friction ×2.0 and start shift −0.6), the edit succeeds 16/16 while open-loop gets 0/16.   • Reading at scale vs. shallow cues (Experiments 1, 6-read, 7): In Experiment 1, a 20-line, 3-rule action-axis script gets 3/4 without an LLM, and under anon+shuffle Astra reaches the same 3-of-4 accuracy as the script, though not on the same games: the script misses Freeway and gets Pac-Man; Astra gets Freeway and abstains on Pac-Man (9/9 on Freeway/Breakout/Pong, 3/3 abstain on Pac-Man). At the 1.5M-weight scale in Experiment 6, fresh blind Astra sessions recover code structure (identifying dense PPO 3/3 and reading empty-board opening biases) but swap b0 and b50 3/3 at 94–99.7% confidence and predict capture, escape, and pairwise win rates no better than without weights (MAE 31.4 vs 28.1, 31.2 vs 31.3, 36.3 vs 34.5 points). In Experiment 7, our weight-distance-to-base check (0/6 discovery, 5/6 confirmation vs a 6/6 bar) is not a reliable classifier and adds no new evidence either way beyond the base model’s 100% wrong-guess following rate. 大规模权重读取与浅层线索（实验 1、6-read、7）：在实验 1 中，20 行 3 规则动作轴脚本无需 LLM 即答对 3/4，而在 anon+shuffle 下 Astra 达到同样的 3/4 准确率，但答对的游戏不同：脚本漏判 Freeway、答对 Pac-Man；Astra 答对 Freeway、在 Pac-Man 上拒答（Freeway/Breakout/Pong 9/9，Pac-Man 3/3 拒答）。在实验 6 的 1.5M 权重规模下，全新的盲测 Astra 会话虽能恢复代码结构特征（3/3 识别稠密 PPO 并读出空盘开局偏置），却在 3/3 次会话中以 94–99.7% 的置信度将 b0 与 b50 颠倒，且对提子率、逃征率及两两胜率的预测误差与不给权重的对照组无异（MAE 分别为 31.4 对 28.1、31.2 对 31.3、36.3 对 34.5 个百分点）。在实验 7 中，我们的基座权重距离检验（探索集 0/6，确认集 5/6 未达 6/6 门槛）并非可靠分类器，除指出基座模型本身即 100% 顺从错误猜测外，未提供新的正反证据。

What holds, what doesn’t哪些结论成立，哪些被高估

Takehi et al. introduce Weight-Space Programming: asking a frontier language model (GPT-6 Astra) to directly inspect, edit, or synthesize neural network weights in natural language or via a Python sandbox without gradient descent. The original abstract (commit 6bfa7a6, 2026-11-02) stated that frontier LLMs read and write weights “well past what we’d expect from humans or conventional algorithms”; on 2026-11-03 (commit e35f074), the authors softened this phrasing to say that these results “using a variety of recent tools and algorithms ... may extend beyond what we’d expect from humans or conventional algorithms.” Takehi 等人 提出了权重空间编程（Weight-Space Programming）：让前沿大语言模型（GPT-6 Astra）在不依赖梯度下降的情况下，通过自然语言或 Python 沙箱直接读取、编辑或从零生成神经网络权重。原版摘要（提交 6bfa7a6，2026-11-02）称前沿 LLM 读写权重的能力 “well past what we’d expect from humans or conventional algorithms”（本文译文：“远超我们对人类或传统算法的预期”）；在 2026-11-03（提交 e35f074），作者将该表述软化为 “using a variety of recent tools and algorithms ... may extend beyond what we’d expect from humans or conventional algorithms”（本文译文：“结合多种近期工具与算法……可能超出我们对人类或传统算法的预期”）。

Because the authors released full prompts, transcripts, checkpoints, and evaluation scripts, we were able to directly re-run their artifacts and test what separates genuine weight-space capability from baseline selection, task simplicity, or standard interpretability recipes. 得益于作者公开了完整的提示词、推理轨迹、模型权重与评测脚本，我们得以直接重跑全部实验产物，并检验其中究竟有多少来自真正的“权重空间编程能力”，又有多少源于基线选择、任务本身的简单性或现有的机械可解释性算法。

Across our checks, we classify each claim into four categories: 在本次审计中，我们将原博客的各项主张分为以下四类：

  • Holds: The claim and its numerical measurements survive our replication and controls. 成立（Holds）：原主张及其数值结果通过了我们的复现检验与对照实验。
  • Overstated: The reported numbers are real and reproducible, but the comparative conclusion depends on an untuned baseline, a handicapped evaluation mode, or a task solvable by a simple non-LLM baseline. 高估（Overstated）：报告的数值真实且可复现，但其对比结论依赖于未调优的基线、受限的评测解码模式，或该任务本身可被简单的非 LLM 基线解决。
  • Contradicted: Direct experimental evidence contradicts the comparative claim as stated. 反驳（Contradicted）：直接的实验数据与原博客所作的对比陈述相矛盾。
  • Untested: Not yet re-run or awaiting completion of multi-seed / multi-reply runs. 未测（Untested）：尚未重跑，或正等待多随机种子／多次采样复现实验完成。

Astra vs. frontier open-weight modelsAstra 与前沿开源权重模型对比

To test whether the small-network reading and compilation tasks in Experiments 1, 2, and 5 are trivial for any frontier LLM, we compared the same prompts on three open-weight reasoning models for Experiments 1, 2, and 5 (DeepSeek V4.1, Kimi K3, and GLM 5.3; exp1/results/scores_open.csv, exp2/score_replies.py, exp5/astra_write/results_high/scores.csv). The capabilities are specific to GPT-6 Astra: the open models are near chance on Experiment 1, hallucinate specific games on random-init weights, fail to read DDPM shapes in Experiment 2, and none of them compiles working weights in Experiment 5. With 1 reply per game at effort high (Astra: xhigh) and each model’s maximum output length, Breakout / Asterix / Seaquest score 7.9 / 1.6 / 0.56 (DeepSeek V4.1), 11.3 / 5.5 / 0.11 (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, a smaller variant), 7.8 / — / 0.64 (Kimi K3) and — / 11.1 / 0.18 (GLM 5.3), against 490 / 62.5 / 33.5 for Astra and 0.51 / 0.51 / 0.12 for random play (— = no result: output cap or dropped connection; exp5/astra_write/results_high/). A first attempt at xhigh failed almost entirely for infrastructure reasons (output caps, dropped connections). In Experiment 5 Breakout (orig), Kimi K3 (198 nonzero weights, 12,988 output tokens) scores 0.48 (n = 1) because although its prose design describes a sensible ball-interception controller, its compilation into tensor indices is wrong—for example, writing W1[156], which is (row 2, col 6, channel 0) (the paddle channel), where its comment says “ball cell” on channel 3. 为检验实验 1、2、5 中的小型网络读取与编译任务对任意前沿大语言模型是否轻而易举，我们在三款开源权重推理模型（DeepSeek V4.1、Kimi K3 与 GLM 5.3；见 exp1/results/scores_open.csv、exp2/score_replies.py、exp5/astra_write/results_high/scores.csv）上对实验 1、2、5 运行了相同提示词。结果显示这些能力为 GPT-6 Astra 所特有：开源模型在实验 1 上接近随机猜测，在随机初始化权重上自信地产生幻觉，在实验 2 中无法读出 DDPM 生成形状，且在实验 5 中无一能编译出有效权重。在每款游戏 1 次、推理强度 high（Astra 为 xhigh）且使用各模型最大输出长度的条件下，Breakout / Asterix / Seaquest 得分分别为 7.9 / 1.6 / 0.56（DeepSeek V4.1）、11.3 / 5.5 / 0.11（DeepSeek V4.1 Flash，较小的变体）、7.8 / — / 0.64（Kimi K3）与 — / 11.1 / 0.18（GLM 5.3），而 Astra 为 490 / 62.5 / 33.5，随机操作为 0.51 / 0.51 / 0.12（— 表示无结果：触及输出上限或连接中断；exp5/astra_write/results_high/）。首轮 xhigh 尝试几乎全部因基础设施原因失败（输出上限、连接中断）。例如在实验 5 的 Breakout（orig）中，Kimi K3（198 个非零权重，12,988 个输出词元）仅得 0.48 分（n = 1）：尽管其文字方案描述了合理的落点拦截控制器，但编译为张量下标时出错——例如将 W1[156]（实际对应第 2 行、第 6 列、通道 0 即挡板通道）误当作注释所写的通道 3“球单元格”。

What we are not claiming我们并非主张什么

Before detailing each experiment, we want to state clearly what this audit does not claim: 在展开各个实验细节之前，我们希望明确说明本次审计并非主张以下观点：

  • The numbers in the original post reproduce. In every experiment we re-executed (Experiments 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 8, plus recomputing the released evaluation logs for Experiment 6), the scores of the authors’ checkpoints and Astra outputs match their reported tables within normal sampling variance. 原博客报告的数值均可复现。在我们重新运行的全部实验中（实验 1、2、3、4、5 和 8，以及对实验 6 评测日志的重新核算），作者提供的模型权重与 Astra 输出结果均在正常采样波动范围内与原文表格吻合。
  • There is no fabrication or data manipulation. The checkpoints, prompts, and Astra transcripts are genuine. In Experiment 1, our anonymized control confirms that Astra reads the weights rather than exploiting prompt leakage (12/12 accuracy). 不存在数据造假或篡改。所有模型检查点、提示词与 Astra 交互记录均真实可信。在实验 1 中，我们的匿名化对照实验证实 Astra 确实读取了权重，而非利用提示词文本泄露（准确率 12/12）。
  • The authors released unusually complete logs. The companion repository (github.com/RikiyaT/lm_understand-logs, commit 4d09ba6) contains prompts, reasoning traces, code executions, checkpoints, and unselected runs (such as the 14 Qwen runs in Experiment 8, the threshold notes in Experiment 3, and the omitted runs in Experiment 6). Several of our critiques rely directly on evidence the authors themselves documented in their release files. 作者公开了非常详尽的实验日志。配套开源仓库（github.com/RikiyaT/lm_understand-logs，提交 4d09ba6）包含了完整的提示词、推理摘要、执行代码、权重文件以及未入选正文的运行记录（例如实验 8 中的 14 次 Qwen 运行、实验 3 的阈值说明以及实验 6 未展示的运行）。我们的多项分析直接建立在作者主动公开的档案之上。
  • Astra shows genuine competence on specific tasks that open-weight models fail. In Experiment 3, Astra correctly chains weight-algebra steps (QK antisymmetry and K- minus Q-composition) and makes better threshold judgements than our fixed pre-registered scripts; in Experiments 1 and 2 it reliably identifies games (12/12) and shapes (9/9) where open models guess near chance; in Experiments 5 and 6 it compiles working Breakout/Asterix controllers and Go networks b0/b50 that beat our 1-ply tactical script 90–95%; and in Experiment 8 its edit beats our automated difference-of-means recipe by +8.8 percentage points on MATH-500 (50.6% vs 41.9%). Our argument is about what is being done (program compilation and shallow/algebraic feature extraction) and how much of the headline gap comes from weak baselines, single-run selection, or source code in the prompt. Astra 在开源模型失败的特定任务上展现出真实能力。在实验 3 中，Astra 正确串联了权重代数分析步骤（QK 反对称性与 K 减 Q 组合分数），并做出了优于我们固定预设脚本的阈值判断；在实验 1 与 2 中，它稳定识别出游戏（12/12）与扩散形状（9/9），而开源模型仅接近随机猜测；在实验 5 与 6 中，它成功编译出有效的 Breakout／Asterix 控制器以及以 90–95% 胜率击败我们 1 步战术脚本的围棋网络 b0/b50；在实验 8 中，其权重编辑在 MATH-500 上比我们的自动化均值差脚本高出 +8.8 个百分点（50.6% 对 41.9%）。我们的核心关切在于：模型究竟在做什么（将给定源码编译为权重，以及提取浅层或代数特征），以及宣传的巨大差距中究竟有多少源于薄弱基线、单次运行筛选或提示词中的源码。

How the Breakout net works (decoded from hand.npz and breakout__orig__r0__gpt-6-astra.json)Breakout 网络的工作原理（解码自 hand.npz 与 breakout__orig__r0__gpt-6-astra.json）
  • Big weights make tanh a binary switch: Weights of magnitude 8 to 1,000 drive \(\tanh(\cdot)\) into saturation at ±1.000, turning smooth hidden units into digital comparators and logic gates (policy entropy 0.000 nats in hand.npz, where 92 edit statements set 815 of 20,486 weights [4%] nonzero and about 50% of episodes hit the 10,000-step cap). 大权重将 tanh 变成二值开关：幅度达 8 至 1,000 的大权重直接将 \(\tanh(\cdot)\) 推入 ±1.000 饱和区，使平滑神经元退化为数字比较器与逻辑门（hand.npz 策略熵为 0.000 nats，92 条赋值语句仅设 20,486 个权重中的 815 个［4%］为非零，约 50% 对局触及 10,000 步上限）。
